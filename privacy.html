IAMSA Membership Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal data IAMSA collects from its members, how and why we use it, and your rights over it, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

IAMSA is a national medical student society for artificial intelligence in healthcare, run as an unincorporated association by its executive committee. IAMSA is the data controller for the personal data you give us when you join.

Contact for any data protection question or request: admin@iamsa.org.

2. What personal data we collect

When you join as a member, we collect your name and email address. We do not collect mobile numbers on the membership sign-up form.

If you apply to become a Regional Lead or International Representative, we additionally collect:

  • Your university (Regional Lead applicants) or country (International Representative applicants)
  • Your written answers describing your leadership experience and how your skills would help you contribute to IAMSA
  • A copy of your CV, uploaded as part of your application

When you upload a file such as your CV, Google separately records the name, email address and profile photo linked to the Google Account you use to sign in, as part of its own file-upload feature. Google processes this on our behalf under the safeguards described in section 4.

We do not ask for, and your CV and application answers should not include, special category data (Article 9 UK GDPR) such as health, religious or political information, unless it is directly relevant to your application.

3. How we use your data and our lawful basis

We use your personal data for the following purposes, each with its own lawful basis under Article 6 UK GDPR:

  • To run your membership, keeping a membership list, confirming your membership, and sending notices about IAMSA business such as meetings, elections or changes to this notice. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) in maintaining a membership list to operate as a society, which members would reasonably expect.
  • To assess and select Regional Leads and International Representatives, using the application details and CV you provide. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) in recruiting volunteers to run IAMSA's regional and international network, which applicants would reasonably expect.
  • To send you our newsletter and news about events, opportunities and IAMSA activities by email. Lawful basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR), given by ticking the opt-in box on the sign-up form. You can withdraw this consent at any time (section 6).

We will not use your personal data for any other purpose without telling you first.

4. Who we share it with

  • Only members of the IAMSA executive committee can access the membership list, each using their own account. Access is removed when someone leaves the committee.
  • We use Google Forms and Google Sheets to collect and store responses. Google processes this data on our behalf as a data processor and may store it outside the UK. Where it does, this is subject to legally recognised safeguards under Article 46 UK GDPR, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or an equivalent addendum to standard contractual clauses.
  • We do not sell, share or give your personal data to anyone else, including sponsors or partners, unless the law requires it.

Optional WhatsApp group. Our newsletter may include a link to an IAMSA WhatsApp group. Joining is entirely your choice. If you join, your name and mobile number may be visible to the group's admins (IAMSA executive committee members) and to other members. WhatsApp processes your data as a separate controller, under its own privacy policy. We do not copy numbers from the group into our membership records, and you can leave the group at any time.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data while you are a member, and delete it 12 months after your membership ends, or sooner if you ask us to. We review the membership list every September, at the start of the academic year, and delete any entries we no longer need.

If your application for Regional Lead or International Representative is unsuccessful, we delete your application answers and CV within 12 months of our decision, or sooner if you ask us to.

6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (Article 15)
  • Have inaccurate data corrected (Article 16)
  • Ask us to erase your data (Article 17)
  • Restrict, or object to, how we use your data (Articles 18 and 21)
  • Withdraw your consent to the newsletter and news emails at any time (Article 7(3)), using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us. This does not affect the lawfulness of anything we did before you withdrew it.

Email admin@iamsa.org to use any of these rights. We will respond within one month, as required by Article 12(3) UK GDPR, extendable by a further two months for complex requests.

7. How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please contact us first at admin@iamsa.org. Under section 164A of the Data Protection Act 2018, we will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay.

If you remain unhappy with our response, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's independent supervisory authority, under section 165 of the Data Protection Act 2018: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

8. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the way we use your personal data changes, and tell members by email.
