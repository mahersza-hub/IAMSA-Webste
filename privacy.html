Privacy

IAMSA Membership Privacy Notice

1. Who we are

IAMSA ("IAMSA", "we") is a national medical student society for artificial intelligence in healthcare. IAMSA is an unincorporated association run by its executive committee. IAMSA is the data controller for the information you give us when you join.

Contact for any data protection question or request: admin@iamsa.org.

2. What we collect

When you join, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address

We do not collect mobile numbers on the sign-up form. We do not ask for, and you should not give us, sensitive information such as health details.

3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To run your membership (keeping a membership list, confirming your membership, and sending notices about IAMSA business such as meetings, elections or changes to this notice). Lawful basis: legitimate interests. IAMSA needs a list of its members to operate as a society, and members would reasonably expect this.
  • To send you our newsletter and news about events, opportunities and IAMSA activities by email. Lawful basis: your consent, given by ticking the opt-in box on the sign-up form. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 6).

We will not use your information for any other purpose without telling you first.

4. Who can see it

  • Only members of the IAMSA executive committee can access the membership list, each using their own account. Access is removed when someone leaves the committee.
  • We use Google Forms and Google Sheets to collect and store responses. Google processes the data on our behalf as a service provider and may store it outside the UK; Google uses legally recognised safeguards for such transfers.
  • We do not sell, share or give your information to anyone else, including sponsors or partners, unless the law requires it.

Optional WhatsApp group. Our newsletter may include a link to an IAMSA WhatsApp group. Joining is your choice. If you join, your name and mobile number can be seen by the group's admins (IAMSA executive committee members) and may be visible to other members. WhatsApp handles your information under its own privacy policy. We do not copy numbers from the group into our membership records, and you can leave the group at any time.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a member. We delete it 12 months after your membership ends, or sooner if you ask us to. We review the membership list every September, at the start of the academic year and delete entries we no longer need.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct it
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Object to us using it, or ask us to restrict how we use it
  • Withdraw your consent to the newsletter and news emails at any time, using the unsubscribe instructions in any email or by emailing admin@iamsa.org. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before.

To use any of these rights, email admin@iamsa.org. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

7. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the way we use your information changes, and tell members by email.
